CONTINUUM C1
3D industrial design concept, v0.1
Design date: October 24, 2026

SCOPE
The assembly models a headset and a desktop storage dock, including internal component envelopes, an exploded configuration and an illustrated signal path. Signal acquisition, digitization, wireless transmission and storage are proposed functions. Reading, encoding, transferring and restoring consciousness are not implemented.

FILES AND UNITS
Open interactive-model.html locally in a WebGL-capable desktop browser. The viewer is self-contained and does not upload data. The assembly and exploded GLB files include component names and metadata; the exploded configuration uses 70% spacing. The OBJ and MTL must stay in the same folder. The model source is c1-model.js; viewer-source.html is a template, not a standalone app. components.json catalogs all 30 parts. The three preview images show the assembly, exploded layout and data path. validation.json records checks on the digital files and viewer, not on physical hardware.

The source, OBJ and catalog use millimeters. The GLBs use meters via a root scale of 0.001. X is left/right, Y is up and +Z points toward the front of the wearer. Catalog boundsMm values give the full width, height and depth (X/Y/Z) of each component. The headset measures approximately 225.1 × 121.5 × 284.7 mm. The dock, including its cradle and exposed ports, measures approximately 240 × 118 × 196.7 mm, with a nominal housing footprint of 240 × 190 mm. None of these dimensions has been validated for fit or manufacture.

PART GROUPS
H01–H12: Headband, liner, crown arch, left and right rails, cable harness, rear enclosure, analog front end/ADC board, controller/radio board, battery pack, adjustment dial and front status strip.
E01–E08: Eight scalp sensing electrode modules.
E09: Reference and bias assembly with two surface contacts.
D01–D09: Lower dock housing, lid, cradle, receiver/encryption board, removable storage, power/network ports, status panel, feet/fasteners and an empty research bay.
A part may contain more than one mesh. The assembly contains 143 meshes and 27,992 triangles.

PROPOSED SIGNAL PATH
1. E01–E08 and E09 provide inputs for measuring scalp potential differences. Performance would depend on the electrodes, contact quality and acquisition electronics.
2. H06 carries the signals to H08. The analog front end would amplify and condition them; the ADC would convert them to digital samples.
3. H09 would timestamp, buffer and transmit the samples wirelessly. H10 would power the headset.
4. D04 would receive, verify and encrypt the data. D05 would hold encrypted recordings.
5. D06 reserves space for the network connection. The cloud icon represents storage for data and model files.
6. D09 is an empty bay. No hardware or software in this model implements whole-brain state readout, consciousness encoding or continuity of subjective experience.
The animated paths and cloud icon illustrate information flow. They are not physical beams, measured signals or a live remote service.

PROPOSED MATERIALS
Housing: PC-ABS. A different printable material is possible for an appearance prototype, but wall thickness, build orientation and joints would need redesign.
Internal support: aluminum alloy.
Headset liner and cradle pads: silicone or foam.
Electrodes: silver/silver chloride contact placeholders. Final contact materials, geometry and pressure require specialist selection.
Circuit boards: FR-4 placeholders. Chip packages, connectors and the battery represent space allowances, not components from a completed electrical bill of materials.

VIEWING AND EDITING
Select Assembly, Exploded view, Transparent housing, Headset fit or How it works. Drag to rotate and scroll to zoom. Inspect a component using the part menu or by clicking its geometry. The data path menu shows five proposed signal-handling steps and a separate, explicitly unimplemented consciousness-reconstruction step. The translucent generic head is not a scan of a real person or evidence of fit.
Import either GLB into a glTF-compatible viewer or modeling application. Specify millimeters when importing the OBJ. createContinuumC1(THREE) returns root, headset, dock, parts and related objects. This release uses Three.js 0.128.0. Source changes do not automatically update exported files: rebuild the viewer and export the model again.

FABRICATION LIMITS
Use the model for appearance review, layout discussions and further mechanical design. It is not a STEP/B-rep engineering model. Bosses, openings, snap fits, assembly tolerances, structural strength, cooling, electromagnetic compatibility and electrical safety have not been fully designed. Adjacent meshes can overlap and have not been boolean-unioned. The package is not a ready-to-slice set of parts.
No PCB schematics, board layout, battery protection circuit, firmware, working encryption software or cloud service is included. Do not connect the placeholder electronics, electrodes or battery to a person. A real acquisition prototype would need appropriate acquisition hardware and engineering review. This design is not for diagnosis, treatment or consciousness transfer.
